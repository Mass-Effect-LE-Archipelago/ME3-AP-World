# Mass Effect Legendary Edition 3

An experimental Archipelago integration for the PC Legendary Edition of Mass Effect 3. It catalogs individual credit caches, supply interactions, armor and weapon-mod pickups, selected weapon interactions, galaxy-map recoveries, story milestones and objective steps.

The first release will use a maximum-content Shepard history. Configurable history is planned later. The current generator uses Crucible Intel as its finale requirement and retains vanilla mission ordering. Native initialization, item delivery and gate enforcement remain in development.
