# Mass Effect Legendary Edition 3

Archipelago support for the PC Legendary Edition of Mass Effect 3 (beta).

## What gets randomized

- **Checks:** credit caches, supply caches, armor and weapon-mod pickups, Mars objective steps, story milestones, codex
  pages and galaxy-map war-asset recoveries (each group can be turned off). Heavy/special weapon pickups are optional.
- **Items:** Crucible Intel, every loadout weapon except the starting M-3 Predator, and credits.

## Goal

Collect the required Crucible Intel. Cronos Station (Cerberus Headquarters) and Earth stay locked on the galaxy map
until you have it; then finish the game.

## How it plays

- The mod skips the Earth prologue: an imported ME2 career starts on the Normandy with Mars available on the galaxy map.
- Your Shepard's ME1/ME2 history is a fixed "maximum content" history. The client writes it as an ME2 career that you
  import when you start ME3, built from your own newest finished ME2 LE save.
- Missed a check on a mission you can't return to? The Normandy's store terminal sells "<mission> Salvage": each
  purchase sends one check you left behind there.
